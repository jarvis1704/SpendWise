# Privacy Policy for SpendWise

**Effective Date:** April 8, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the SpendWise app (hereby referred to as "Application") for mobile devices that was created by Biprangshu (hereby referred to as "Service Provider") as a Free service. This service is intended for use "AS IS".

## Information Collection and Use

The Application collects and processes certain information to provide and improve the Service.

### 1. Locally Stored Data
The primary function of SpendWise is to manage and track your financial transactions.
*   **Financial Data:** All your transaction data, budgets, and financial records are stored **locally** on your device using a local database (Room Database). The Service Provider does not have access to your personal financial records.
*   **App Preferences:** Your settings, such as themes, notification preferences, and budget goals, are stored locally on your device.

### 2. Biometric Information
The Application offers biometric authentication (e.g., Fingerprint or Face Unlock) to secure your financial data.
*   The Application utilizes your device's native biometric API.
*   **Biometric data never leaves your device** and is never shared with or stored by the Service Provider or any third-party services.

### 3. Third-Party Services
The Application uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you. These services are used to facilitate certain features, analyze app usage, and improve the user experience.

The Application uses the following third-party services:
*   **Google Play Services:** For core Android functionalities and app distribution.
*   **Google Analytics for Firebase:** Used to track app usage, user engagement, and help identify bugs or crashes. This service may collect data such as device type, operating system version, and general usage events.
*   **Firebase AI:** To provide intelligent categorization, insights, or other AI-powered features within the Application.

For more information on how these services handle your data, please refer to their respective privacy policies:
*   [Google Play Services Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
*   [Google Analytics for Firebase Privacy Policy](https://firebase.google.com/policies/analytics)

## Data Retention and Deletion

Since your financial data is stored locally on your device, you retain full control over it. You can delete all your data at any time by clearing the Application's data in your device settings or by uninstalling the Application. 

If you uninstall the Application, all locally stored financial records will be permanently deleted unless you have manually created a backup.

## Security

The Service Provider values your trust in providing your information. The Application uses secure local storage and relies on the Android operating system's sandbox environment to protect your data. However, remember that no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure and reliable, and the Service Provider cannot guarantee its absolute security.
